# Week 3 answer key: Understanding investment risk

**Admin only. Do not post this file in the group.** Share the answers after people have had a chance to reply and discuss.

1. **a) How much an investment's price moves up and down.** Volatility measures how much prices fluctuate. It does not tell you what you will lose or earn in the end.

2. **b) A temporary decline.** The fall was temporary because the investment recovered and you did not sell at the low point. It would only have become a permanent loss if you had sold low, or if it had never recovered.

3. **c) A bumpier path and a wider range of outcomes.** Higher volatility means bigger ups and downs along the way and more uncertainty about the final result, which could end up higher or lower.
