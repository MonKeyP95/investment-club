# Week 2 answer key: Inflation and purchasing power

**Admin only. Do not post this file in the group.** Share the answers after people have had a chance to reply and discuss.

1. **c) €5.40.** Inflation compounds, just like returns. At 3% a year, €3.00 becomes about €4.03 after 10 years and about €5.42 after 20.

2. **b) It shrinks.** Your balance grows by 2%, but prices grow by 3%. In real terms you lose about 1% of buying power each year.

3. **a) What your money can actually buy.** The number of euros you have is the nominal value. Purchasing power is what those euros buy.
